# Basis Rotation, Lie Algebra, and Operator Growth

For complete derivations, JVP equations, and loss definitions, see [`theory.md`](theory.md) and [`loss.md`](loss.md). This document details Lie algebra generator properties, Pauli string conjugation, circuit geometry, and operator expansion.

---

## 1. Generator Lie Algebra and Spectral Normalization

Every unitary gate $U_g(\theta_g) = e^{-i \theta_g G_g}$ is generated by a Hermitian operator $G_g = G_g^\dagger$. To ensure consistent angle scaling across single-spin and multi-spin gates, every generator is normalized to spectral norm $\|G_g\|_2 = 1/2$.

| Family | Generator $G$ | Lie Algebra Properties | Spectral Norm $\|G\|_2$ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Primitive Pauli | $P / 2$ | Single or composite Pauli string | $1/2$ |
| `givens` | $(X_i Y_j - Y_i X_j)/4$ | Real-orthogonal, conserves total $S^z$ | $1/2$ |
| `xy_exchange` | $(X_i X_j + Y_i Y_j)/4$ | Conserves total $S^z$, complex exchange | $1/2$ |
| `heisenberg` | $(X_i X_j + Y_i Y_j + Z_i Z_j)/6$ | $SU(2)$ spin-exchange symmetric | $1/2$ |
| `string_givens` | $(X_i Z_k Y_j - Y_i Z_k X_j)/4$ | Real-orthogonal, 3-site support, conserves $S^z$ | $1/2$ |

## 2. Analytic Conjugation & Exact Operator Growth

For Pauli string $P \in \mathcal{P}_N$ and normalized generator $G$ with $G^2 = I$:

$$
e^{i \alpha G} P e^{-i \alpha G} = P + i \sin(2\alpha) G P + (1 - \cos(2\alpha)) G P G.
$$

If $[G, P] = 0$, $e^{i \alpha G} P e^{-i \alpha G} = P$.
If $\{G, P\} = 0$, $e^{i \alpha G} P e^{-i \alpha G} = P \cos(2\alpha) + i G P \sin(2\alpha)$.

Conjugating $H = \sum_P c_P P$ string-by-string through circuit depth $d$ generates $H_\theta = U_\theta^\dagger H U_\theta$. Terms with $|c_P(\theta)| < \text{theta\_cutoff}$ ($10^{-12}$) are pruned. Operator expansion is monitored via:

$$
g_{\text{op}} = \frac{\sum_{P \notin \operatorname{supp}(H)} |c_P(\theta)|^2}{\sum_{P \in \operatorname{supp}(H)} |c_P(0)|^2}.
$$

## 3. Subspace Refresh & Retained-State Selection ($f_{\text{retain}}$)

At each outer adaptive step:
1. **Subspace Freezing ($f_{\text{retain}} = 0.1$)**: Retains the top $K_{\text{retain}} = \lfloor f_{\text{retain}} \cdot M \rfloor$ states from the previous Ritz subspace, ranked by $|v_{0,s}|^2$.
2. **Fresh Seed Sampling**:
   - **Frequency Sampling (`SubspaceSampling.FREQUENCY`, default)**: Hard-capped Born draw batch ($N_{\text{seed\_samples}} = 2048$).
   - **Gumbel Sampling (`SubspaceSampling.GUMBEL`)**: Exact without-replacement top-$K$ beam search.
3. **Epstein-Nesbet Connected Completion**: Ranks connected states by $\eta_y = \frac{|\langle y | H_\theta | v_0 \rangle|^2}{H_{yy} - \epsilon_0}$.
4. **Gumbel Fallback**: Fills any remaining budget shortfall up to $M$.
